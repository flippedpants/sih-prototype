The complainant Benjamin Kapadia states that on 21-07-2026, an unknown person using mobile number +918300412785 contacted the complainant claiming to be from the customer support division of a telecom/banking service and requested an OTP for 'verification purposes'. The complainant, believing the call to be genuine, shared the OTP, following which Rs. 113,786 was debited from the complainant's account without authorization. The amount was traced to have been credited to an account with Bharat Savings Bank. The matter is reported to Hyderabad Cyber Cell Police Station for investigation.
